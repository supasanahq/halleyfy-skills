# Fashion and beauty images

For campaign images, editorials, lookbooks, beauty close-ups and outfit try-on.

## Write it like a creative brief

Start with the brand feeling and audience, then the model, styling, set, light and camera. "Streetwear drop for a Gen Z audience, playful and cool" sets a direction that adjectives alone don't.

- **Model:** the age range, look, hair, makeup and expression the user wants. Invented people only, never real or recognizable ones.
- **Styling:** each garment with its fabric, fit and color. "Oversized lilac puffer jacket, wide khaki cargo pants, chunky white sneakers."
- **Set:** one strong location or a seamless backdrop color.
- **Light:** name it as a photographer would: "single hard key from upper right", "soft overcast daylight", "one spotlight circle on a black floor".
- **Camera:** framing and lens feel: "extreme close-up, 100mm macro", "full body, 35mm, low angle".

## One striking element

What makes a campaign image stop the scroll is usually one surprising, physical element: liquid gold running down a cheek like jewelry, clothes floating in zero gravity, a wave curling behind a gown. Describe it as a real thing with material and light. One element, not three.

```text
High-end beauty campaign photograph: extreme close-up of a woman in her mid-twenties with dewy "glass skin" and sleek wet-look black hair, looking straight into the lens.
Rivulets of liquid gold run from her cheekbone down her jaw like molten jewelry, with sharp highlights.
Deep teal seamless backdrop, one hard key light from the upper right, real skin texture with visible pores, medium-format camera, 100mm macro.
Her face on the right half of the frame; the left side falls into soft shadow. No text, no logos.
```

## Beauty close-ups

Ask for "real skin texture" and "visible pores" or the face turns plastic. Name the makeup precisely (glossy lip, soft brown liner) and where the light catches it.

## Outfit try-on and product on a model

Pass the person as Image 1 and the garment or product as Image 2, then: "Dress the woman in Image 1 in the jacket from Image 2. Do not change her face, skin tone, body, hair or pose. Match the original lighting." Change one garment per run.

## Composition for hero banners

When text or UI will sit on one side, put the face on the other third and let that side fall into shadow or plain backdrop.
