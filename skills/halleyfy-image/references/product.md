# Product images

For packshots, hero shots, lifestyle scenes and catalog images of a physical product.

## With or without the real product

- **The user has a product photo:** pass it as `--image` (Image 1) and lock it: "Image 1 is the product: keep its shape, proportions, cap, colors and label text exactly." A clean, evenly lit, front-on photo works best. Several angles help: pass them as Images 1-3 and say so.
- **The photo is cluttered, dark or shows only part of the product:** first make a clean packshot from it ("Change only the background to a plain off-white seamless backdrop; keep the product exactly"), check it, then pass the clean packshot as Image 1 and the original photo as Image 2 "for detail". A clear photo can go in directly.
- **No photo (concept or made-up product):** describe it like a spec sheet: form, material, finish, color, cap or closure, and the exact label text in quotes. Keep that description word for word for every later image (see `consistency.md`).

## Hero shot

One product, one idea, nothing competing.

- Surface and backdrop: "wet slab of pale travertine", "seamless deep-orange backdrop".
- Light: a key and a rim, plus "soft contact shadow" so it sits on the surface instead of floating.
- Camera: at product height for a confident, heroic look; a slight top-down angle for food or flat items.
- Leave room for copy: "top 40% left empty".

```text
Use: hero image for a skincare launch ad.
Image 1 is the product: keep its shape, cap, colors and label exactly.
Scene: the bottle stands on a wet slab of pale travertine; a thin film of water reflects it.
Composition: bottle centered in the lower third, camera at bottle height, 85mm look, label sharp, top 40% empty for a headline.
Light: large softbox from the left, thin rim light from back right, soft contact shadow, true-to-life color.
Text: only the existing label. No other text, logos, props or watermark.
Photorealistic commercial product photograph.
```

## Lifestyle scene

Show the moment of use: a real place, time of day, the hand or person using it, a few props that tell the story. Keep the product the sharpest thing in frame and true to the reference.

## Catalog and marketplace

- Plain seamless white or light grey backdrop, even shadowless light, product filling about 80% of the frame, 1:1.
- halleyfy can't make transparent PNGs yet. For a cutout, ask for a flat white backdrop and remove it afterwards, or tell the user.

## Effects that sell (one per image)

A frozen splash, the product rising out of its main ingredient, a cross-section, a levitating exploded view. Describe each effect physically: what it is made of, its shape and direction, where it sits relative to the product, how the light hits it. Effects named in a word ("with a splash") come out generic; effects directed in detail look shot.

## Check before handing over

The label text is spelled right, the shape matches the reference, the product isn't merged into the surface, no stray logos.
