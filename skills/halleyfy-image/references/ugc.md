# UGC and phone-photo images

For pictures that should look like a real person took them on a phone: creator posts, reviews, testimonial stills, the first frame of a UGC video ad.

## The core move

Ask for an ordinary photo, not an ad. Say it outright: "an ordinary phone photo a real customer might post, not an advertisement." Then pick 3-5 realism cues, not all of them:

- Taken at arm's length on a phone, slight front-camera wide angle.
- Mixed indoor light, or window daylight, or direct phone flash.
- Mild noise, slightly off-center or tilted framing.
- Real clutter: a toothbrush cup, a hair tie, a half-open drawer.
- Real skin texture, visible pores, short unpolished nails, hair slightly messy.

Leave out "cinematic", "studio lighting", "color graded", "bokeh": they pull it back toward an ad.

## With the user's product

Pass the product photo as Image 1 and lock it. Make the product big in frame and front-facing, held by the person, label toward the camera. In a phone photo it should look handled, not placed.

```text
Image 1: the hand cream tube. Keep its shape, colors and label text exactly.
An ordinary phone photo a real customer might post: a woman in her late twenties holds the tube up beside her face in her bathroom, label toward the camera, slightly messy sink behind her (toothbrush cup, hair tie).
Shot at arm's length, mild wide-angle distortion, overhead light mixed with window daylight, mild noise, slightly off-center.
Real skin texture, short unpolished nails. Not an ad: no studio light, no color grading, no added text or watermark.
```

## People

- Describe the person the user asked for: age range, look, clothing, expression. Don't use real or recognizable people.
- Natural, mid-moment expressions sell UGC: mid-sentence, laughing, looking at the product. A posed smile reads as stock.

## For a UGC video

Make this still at the video's aspect (9:16 for reels and TikTok, 16:9 for YouTube), with the person framed chest-up, the face fully in frame and room around the hands. Read `start-frames.md` too.
